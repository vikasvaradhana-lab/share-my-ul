1. Executive Summary & Core Concept

Share My UL is an automated, web-based ticket sharing application designed for students in Uppsala. It allows students in a private WhatsApp group to view real-time availability of an unused UL transit season ticket, select exact 12-hour or 24-hour time slots, and submit instant booking requests over WhatsApp without needing to register user accounts.

💡 Why It Was Built

To monetize and share season ticket downtime with student friends at affordable rates (25 SEK for 12h, 30 SEK for 24h) while automating scheduling, timezone math, and awake handover constraints.

🚀 100% Free Stack ($0/month)

Leveraged permanent free tiers: Vercel Edge Serverless Hosting, Supabase Managed PostgreSQL, Google Cloud OAuth 2.0, and GitHub Cloud Repositories.

3. Key Business Rules & Timezone Engine

  • Three-Timezone Architecture:
    • Storage: UTC ISO timestamps in database.
    • Display: Stockholm local time (Europe/Stockholm) with automatic summer/winter daylight saving time.
    • Admin Awake Constraints: Verified against Indian Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata 06:30–22:30 IST) for both start and end handover moments.
  • Interactive Slider: 30-minute increment range slider with live duration, price, and handover validation.
  • Cross-Midnight Window: Merges contiguous available slots across consecutive green days for 24h slots.
  • Timeline Color Rules:
    • 🟢 Available: Active bookable periods.
    • 🩷 Reserved for me: Personal travel days/slots.
    • 🔴 Reserved: Booked student slots.
    • Past / Unbookable: Elapsed earlier hours of today or dates past booking cutoff.

4. Admin Portal Features (`/admin`)

📅 Schedule Accordion & Smart Conflict Overwrite

Month filter tabs, auto-collapsed rows, and smart conflict resolution: 1-click 🔄 Replace Overlapping Blocks & Save to overwrite existing spans without manual deletion.

🩷 Personal Use Booking Calendar

Visual monthly calendar in Settings for 1-click personal travel reservation. Supports quick presets (All Day, Work Hours 08:30–17:30, Afternoon), custom time pickers, and real-time pink badge indicators.

📊 Visits & Traffic Analytics

Live in-app visitor counters (Total Link Visits, Today's Visits, WhatsApp Clicks) and activity log stored permanently in Supabase PostgreSQL, plus Vercel Web Analytics integration.

🎟️ Bookings & Custom Revenue Editing

Automatic tracking of all shares with smart multi-day rate calculations. Allows inline editing of amounts received (including waived amounts), custom notes, and 1-tap "Mark Completed".
